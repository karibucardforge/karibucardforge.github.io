# Privacy Policy

**Karibu Card Forge**
Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Karibu Card Forge ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our internal application, **KaribuCardForge-Automation** (the "App"), which uses the Pinterest API.

## 1. About the App

The App is a private automation tool operated by Karibu Card Forge. It is used to create and schedule Pins on our own Pinterest business account to promote our trading card products. The App is not offered to the public, and it does not provide any service to other Pinterest users.

## 2. Information the App accesses

Through the Pinterest API, and only with authorisation from the account owner, the App may access:

- Basic details of our own Pinterest account (such as account name and ID)
- Our own boards, so that Pins can be saved to the right place
- Our own Pins, in order to create, schedule and manage them
- Basic performance data for our own Pins (such as impressions and saves), if reporting is enabled

The App does **not** collect, access or store personal information about other Pinterest users, such as their profiles, messages, followers or browsing activity.

## 3. How we use information

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used only to:

- Publish and schedule Pins on our own account
- Organise Pins onto our own boards
- Review the performance of our own content

We do not use this information for advertising profiling, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties.

## 4. Storage and security

The only credential the App stores is the Pinterest access token for our own account, along with any content (images, titles, descriptions, links) we prepare for Pins. This is kept on systems controlled by Karibu Card Forge, and access is limited to the account owner. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

## 5. Data retention

We keep the access token only for as long as the App needs it. Pin content and performance data are kept only as long as they are useful for running our own marketing. We delete the access token if the App is retired or if we revoke its access to Pinterest.

## 6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade information obtained through the Pinterest API. Information is disclosed only to Pinterest itself (as part of using its API) or where we are required to do so by law.

## 7. Your choices and deletion requests

The App only handles data from our own Pinterest account. If you believe the App holds any information about you, or you have any questions, contact us and we will review and delete it where appropriate. The account owner can also revoke the App's access at any time from the Pinterest account settings under "Apps" / "Permissions".

## 8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in their country).

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
